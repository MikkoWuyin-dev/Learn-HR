Your pricing questions, answered.

What if the doctor finds isotretinoin unsuitable?

An assessment does not guarantee eligibility or a prescription. The independent doctor will review your circumstances and discuss appropriate alternatives or next steps. The care fee is not a promise of isotretinoin treatment or results. Applicable refund and cancellation terms will be confirmed and disclosed before any future checkout.

What are the refund and cancellation terms?

A refund or cancellation policy has not yet been confirmed. Confirmed terms, including what happens if treatment is unsuitable or care is cancelled, will be disclosed before checkout. This demo has no checkout and takes no payments.

Which lab tests will I need, and who do I pay?

Your clinician will determine which tests and monitoring are required for your circumstances and explain the timing. Lab tests are not included in the care fee: you pay the lab separately. No fixed testing schedule or fasting instruction is provided by this demo.

Do I pay for medication separately?

Yes. Medication is not included. If a licensed clinician issues a prescription, medication is purchased separately from a licensed retail pharmacy. Delivery is also excluded. There is no automatic prescription or medication purchase in this demo.

What about pregnancy safety and monitoring?

Isotretinoin carries serious risks in pregnancy. Where relevant, care must follow clinician-approved pregnancy-prevention and monitoring protocols, with individual counselling, testing and follow-up directed by the clinician. These protocols require approval before a live service; this demo provides no medical clearance or safety assessment.

Are three consultations always enough?

No. The first three-month fee includes one independent doctor consultation per month. Additional doctor reviews and monitoring may be clinically required and may entail additional charges, disclosed before the extra care is agreed. Visit frequency and treatment duration are clinician-directed, not fixed by the package.

Are the renewal and post-treatment prices final?

No. The proposed ₦55,000 renewal for months 4–6 and ₦20,000 post-treatment review are indicative / subject to confirmation. They are planning proposals, not final published prices or services available to buy here. A six-month course and post-treatment review are not assumed to be appropriate for everyone.
